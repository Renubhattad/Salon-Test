Common Questions

FAQ

Do I need to book in advance?

We strongly recommend booking ahead, especially for colour services and weekend appointments. Our stylists can book out 2–4 weeks in advance. Walk-ins are welcome when availability allows — call your nearest salon to check.

How long does a balayage take?

A full balayage including toner and blowdry typically takes 3–4 hours. We recommend arriving with clean, dry hair for best results. Your stylist will give you a more accurate time estimate at consultation.

What's your cancellation policy?

We ask for at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule. Late cancellations (under 24 hours) or no-shows may be subject to a fee of up to 50% of the service value. We understand emergencies happen — please call us and we'll do our best to help.

Do you offer consultations before booking?

Yes — we offer complimentary 15-minute consultations for new clients considering colour services. You can book a consultation through our online booking system or by calling your nearest salon directly.

Can I bring children to my appointment?

We welcome children for haircuts, but as our salons contain chemicals and tools, we ask that young children are accompanied by a second adult who can supervise them during your appointment. Our Shoreditch and Chelsea locations have a small waiting area.

Do you do gift vouchers?

Absolutely. Gift vouchers are available in any denomination, both in-salon and online. They make the perfect gift and are valid for 12 months from the date of purchase. Email hello@juicesalons.com to order.
