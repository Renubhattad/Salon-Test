Our Story

About JUICE

How It Began

Born from a love
of craft.

JUICE was founded in 2012 by Sophie Laurent, a colourist who had trained at the Vidal Sassoon Academy and worked backstage at London Fashion Week for a decade. Frustrated by salons that prioritised throughput over talent, she opened a single room in Chelsea with five chairs and a singular mission: to do extraordinary work, one client at a time.

Word spread quickly. Within two years, the Chelsea salon was fully booked weeks in advance. By 2016, we'd opened our second location in Shoreditch — a space that married industrial architecture with the warm, intimate atmosphere that had come to define the JUICE experience.

Today, we operate six flagship locations across London and a team of 48 master stylists, colourists, and therapists. We've won 14 industry awards. But nothing has changed about the way we approach our work: with obsessive attention, genuine care, and an artist's eye for what makes each person uniquely beautiful.

What We Believe

Craft First

Every technique we offer is something we've studied, refined, and genuinely mastered. We invest relentlessly in education and only offer services we can execute at the highest level.

Client-Centred

Your appointment is never rushed. We begin every visit with a consultation and end only when you're genuinely happy. Your satisfaction is the only metric we measure ourselves by.

Sustainable Practice

We use only professional-grade, cruelty-free products. Our salons have achieved carbon-neutral status, and we donate 1% of every service to environmental restoration projects.

Inclusive Space

JUICE welcomes everyone. Our team is trained to work with all hair types, textures, and identities. We believe great hair is a universal right, not a privilege.
